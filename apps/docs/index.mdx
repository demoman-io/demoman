---
title: "Introduction"
description: "Demoman is a video recorder for coding agents and a CI pipeline that keeps every video current."
---

A product demo is a static artifact of a dynamic system: the app changes, the
recording does not. Demoman treats a demo as a build artifact instead.

- **The recorder.** Ask your coding agent for a demo of a flow. The agent
  drives Demoman through its MCP server, Demoman runs the flow in a headless
  browser on your machine, and you get a video with the camera, cursor and
  framing already done.
- **The pipeline.** Each flow is saved as a DemoScript in `.demoman/flows/`.
  Commit it, and CI records the flow again when the product changes,
  replacing the video only when the picture changed.

Demoman runs on macOS and x86_64 Linux, with Claude Code, Codex, Cursor,
OpenCode or any MCP agent. It needs no account.
