---
title: "Introduction"
description: "Demoman is a screen recorder for your coding agent."
---

Ask your coding agent for a demo of a flow, such as "make a demo of the new
checkout", and Demoman drives the app in a browser of its own, out of your
way, records it,
and saves the video to `~/Downloads`.
