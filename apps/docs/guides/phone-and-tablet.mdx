---
title: "Phone and tablet recording"
description: "Ask for a demo at phone or tablet size."
---

> "Make a demo of the checkout on a phone."

> "Record it on a tablet."

Demoman records the app in a phone or tablet window, so the video shows what
a person on that device sees.

- **A phone shows a different page.** Menus fold away and layouts change.
  The agent looks at the phone version of the page before it plans the
  steps, so it does not reach for a button the phone does not have.
- **It comes out portrait.** A phone demo is a tall video. Ask for 9:16 to
  be sure.
- **There is no hover on a phone.** The agent taps instead.

Anything you can ask of a desktop demo works here too: a format, a brand
kit, the [studio](/studio/editing) afterwards.
