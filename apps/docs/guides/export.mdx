Ask your agent, or use the studio's Export buttons.

> "Export it as a 9:16 MP4."

> "Make a GIF of it."

## Formats

| Ask for | You get |
| --- | --- |
| MP4 | A video the shape of your screen, the default. Ask for 16:9 to fix the shape |
| 9:16 | A portrait MP4, for phone feeds |
| GIF | An animated GIF |
| Stills | A folder of PNG images, one per step |

## Size

Exports are sized for sharing: 1920 pixels on the long side (1920×1080 at
16:9), or 720×1280 at 9:16, at 30 frames a second. That keeps the file small
enough to attach to a pull request or a message.

For the largest file, ask for full quality, or choose Full under Export in
the studio's Settings.

To keep a file under a size, say how big it may be.

> "Export it under 10 MB."

Demoman lowers the frame rate first, then the size, then the quality, and
says what it lowered.

## Where files land

In `~/Downloads`. Your agent tells you the path when it is done.

To save somewhere else, connect your agent with the stdio server below,
which adds `--out-dir`:

```json
{"command": "npx", "args": ["-y", "demoman@latest", "mcp", "--out-dir", "<folder>"]}
```
