---
title: "Recording frame rate"
description: "Record your app's own motion at 60 frames a second."
---

Demoman records your app at 30 frames a second. Exports are 60, and the
camera and pointer move at 60, but what your app animates (a card sliding
into a column, a dialog opening, a toast) changes on every other frame. At
60, it is smooth too.

## Set it for a repository

Commit a `.demoman/config.json`:

```json
{
  "recordingFrameRate": 60
}
```

It is `30` or `60`, and `30` when it is not set. Any other value, or a file
that is not JSON, stops the run with a sentence naming the file; it is never
read as 30.

You can also set it for yourself in `~/.demoman/settings.json`, and the
repository's file wins. CI reads only the repository's file, so set it there
for videos CI keeps current.

The run report says what each video was recorded at, and where that came
from.

## What it costs

Measured on a 12-second flow:

| Frame rate | Recording time | Disk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 30 | about 15 s | about 9 MB |
| 60 | about 30 s | about 15 MB |

A recording at 60 takes about twice as long and about 60% more disk. You pay
that on every refresh of every flow, so on CI it is twice the minutes.

## Switching

A refresh after you switch records at the new rate, and counts a different
rate as a change. Every flow's video is replaced on its next refresh, each
with the reason: `recorded at 60 fps, the committed video at 30`. Switching
back replaces them again.

## When a video is still 30

- **Exports at 30.** Sharing quality, and an export kept under a size, are
  30 frames a second. A video recorded at 60 exports correctly; the extra
  frames go unused.
- **A visible window, a phone or a tablet.** These record in real time, at
  the screen's own pace, whatever the setting. The run report says that 60
  was set and not applied.
