---
title: "Editing"
description: "Trim, split, mask and restyle a take."
---

Open a take in the studio (see [Overview](/studio/overview)), then:

- **Trim.** Drag the ends of the clip on the timeline.
- **Split.** Press ⌘B, or click the scissors, to split the clip at the
  playhead. Click a piece and press Delete to remove it: the video closes up
  behind it. Drag a piece's inner edge inward to cut frames, or outward to
  bring back frames removed beside it.
- **Restore.** A dot above the clip marks each join. Click it to see what
  was removed, and Restore it.
- **Steps.** Each step is labelled on the timeline. To take one out, split
  the clip on both sides of it and remove that piece. To change what a step
  does, ask your agent to change the script and record it again.
- **Mask.** Click + Mask to cover part of the picture with an opaque box for
  a time range, for example to hide a customer's name.
- **Cursor.** Style, motion, and hiding it. Camera blur and Smoothness are
  under Camera.
- **Background.** The surface behind the app window.
- **Padding.** The space around the window.
- **Zoom.** Where the camera moves in on a step.
- **Aspect ratio.** Auto, 16:9 or 9:16.
- **Brand kit.** A saved look, with your logo, to apply to a take.
