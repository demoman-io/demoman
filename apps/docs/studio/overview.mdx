The Studio is a window where you look over a video, change it, and export
it. Your agent offers it after a run. Or open it yourself:

```bash
npx demoman studio
```

Name a video to open it directly:

```bash
npx demoman studio <video>
```

Close the Studio from the terminal:

```bash
npx demoman studio --stop
```

Your flows, their sizes and your brand kits are listed in the Studio's
Flows and Settings pages. When you are done editing, export from the
studio, or ask your agent to. See [Export](/guides/export).

## Flows, videos and exports

A **flow** is something in your app you want to show, like "Create an
invoice". Each time Demoman films it you get a new **video** of that flow,
numbered 1, 2, 3. Editing a video in the Studio changes that video; filming
again adds a new one. The Flows page shows each flow with its newest video.
After 30 days Demoman frees the footage of older videos you never edited or
exported from; they stay listed as expired and can be filmed again from
their script. Deleting a flow deletes all its videos. Files you exported
are never touched.

Your agent names the flow when it films. Open **videos** on a flow's card to
see each one, its date and the files exported from it. A file that was moved
or deleted is marked gone. Rename a flow by its name on the card. Delete on
the card deletes the whole flow; Delete on a video's row deletes that video.

To delete many flows at once, tick the box in a card's corner (it shows on
hover), then tick the others, or Shift-click to tick a range. **Delete** in
the bar above the list asks once. A flow that cannot go, such as one being
exported, stays ticked and is named with the reason.

An expired video keeps its steps and says how to ask your agent to film it
again. See [Data](/security/data).
